Privacy policy

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the open-source tool rclone, to copy the owner's own files to and from the owner's own Google Drive.

  • No personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to transfer the owner's files.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than the file transfers the owner requests.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
